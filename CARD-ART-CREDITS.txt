Card face artwork attribution
=============================

Artist: David Bellot
Source deck: SVG Cards, https://svg-cards.sourceforge.net/
Wikimedia Commons source category:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_playing_cards_4
License: GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL), as identified on the
individual Wikimedia Commons file pages. Attribution is required.
License information: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl.html

The PNG images in assets/cards/ are 250-pixel-wide Wikimedia Commons
thumbnails rendered from David Bellot's SVG card files. The filenames use
standard rank/suit codes (for example, JD.png is the Jack of Diamonds).
The images are bundled with this project; retain this notice when
redistributing them and follow the LGPL terms.
